## Zoo-Museum (ZooMu) Network -  GBIF Network Summary

**The ZooMu Network is funded by an NSF Research Coordination Networks Grant (RCN) to build a lasting network of biological collections staff** who are equipped to work between collection types, and will open broad new opportunities for biological collections research by linking living and preserved animal collections, databases, and professionals.

**The ZooMu Network GBIF Network** provides data on current and historical data of animals that were housed at zoos, aquariums, and other captive facitilies while they were housed at these institutions, as well as records of natural history museum specimens that originated from zoos, aquariums, and other living animal collections.

To read more about the project, see our BioScience paper: [**Bridging the Research Gap between Live Collections in Zoos and Preserved Collections in Natural History Museums**](https://academic.oup.com/bioscience/article/72/5/449/6570825)
